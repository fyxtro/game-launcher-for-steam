## Description
Game Launcher for Steam turns your Stream Deck into a launcher for your Steam library. Press one key and your whole deck fills with your favorite, installed or recently played games, each shown with its own artwork. Press a game to start it.

Features:
• One key opens the game grid on your whole deck
• Switch between Favorites, Installed, Recently played and All
• Use Steam Favorites or any of your own Steam collections
• Sort by last played or by name
• Finds games on every Steam library drive
• Favorites you have not installed show in grey; press one to install it
• Returns to your previous profile after launching a game (optional)
• Page through large libraries with Previous and Next keys

Setup: drag "Open Game Launcher" onto any key and press it. Stream Deck asks once to install the included profile. You can also build your own layout with the Game Slot, Previous Page, Next Page and Filter & Settings actions.

Everything is read locally from your Steam installation. No Steam login or API key is needed. Requires Steam on Windows 10 or 11 and Stream Deck 7.1 or later. Includes profiles for Stream Deck (15 keys), Stream Deck Mini and Stream Deck XL.
